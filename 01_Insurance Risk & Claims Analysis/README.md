---

## 📊 Data Source
- **File Type:** Excel (.xlsx)
- **Description:** The dataset contains policyholder and claim details consolidated from multiple sources into a single Excel file for analysis in Power BI.

---

## 🧩 Business Requirement
An insurance company aims to better understand its **policyholder base** and **claim patterns** to make data-driven business decisions.  
Currently, policy and claims data are scattered across multiple sources, making it difficult for stakeholders to track performance and identify trends.

The company requires a **centralized interactive dashboard** in Power BI that provides a clear overview of key metrics and visual insights.

---

## 🎯 KPI Requirements
| KPI | Description |
|-----|--------------|
| **Total Policies** | Measures the size of the active customer base. |
| **Total Claim Amount** | Tracks the overall financial impact of claims. |
| **Claim Frequency** | Analyzes how often claims are being made. |
| **Average Claim Amount** | Assesses claim severity and potential risk exposure. |
| **Gender-wise Total Policies** | Understands customer distribution across genders for better segmentation and targeting. |

---

## 📈 Chart Requirements
To go beyond KPIs, the dashboard includes charts that analyze various aspects of insurance policies and claims.  
These visualizations help stakeholders identify risk factors, understand customer segments, and optimize policy decisions.

Dynamic measures used:
- **Total Claim Amount**
- **Total Policies**

---

## 🧮 Visualization Details

| Visualization | Type | Purpose |
|----------------|------|----------|
| **By Car Use** | Donut Chart | Analyzes policy distribution and claim amounts based on car usage (Private vs Commercial). |
| **By Car Make** | Bar Chart | Identifies car brands with higher policies and claims, highlighting brand-based risks. |
| **By Coverage Zone** | Donut Chart | Evaluates policies and claims by geographic zones for regional risk analysis. |
| **By Age Group** | Histogram | Assesses policyholders’ age distribution and identifies which age brackets file more claims. |
| **By Car Year** | Area Chart | Shows how the car’s manufacturing year impacts policy counts and claim amounts. |
| **By Kids Driving** | Ribbon Chart | Compares the impact of young drivers in households on policy count and claim amounts. |
| **By Education** | Pie Chart | Understands how education levels correlate with insurance policy adoption and claims. |
| **By Education & Marital Status** | Matrix Heat Grid | Explores the combined effect of education and marital status on policies and claims. |

---

## 🧠 Dashboard Insights
- **Total Policies:** 37,542  
- **Total Claim Amount:** $187.8M  
- **Claim Frequency:** 0.5  
- **Average Claim Amount:** $5K  
- **Gender Split:** Male – 18.7K | Female – 18.8K  

### Key Observations
- Private car usage contributes the majority of claim amounts.  
- Ford and Chevrolet lead in claim totals among car brands.  
- Claims are evenly distributed across coverage zones.  
- Age groups 26–45 show the highest claim activity.  
- Cars manufactured between 2010–2018 have the highest claim amounts.  
- Households with no kids driving show significantly higher claim totals.  
- Bachelor’s degree holders form the largest segment of policyholders.  
- Married individuals with Bachelor’s degrees contribute the highest claim amounts.
